What We Teach

Six crafts. One creator.

Each course is a project — not a lesson. Children leave with something they made entirely by themselves.

Scratch · Coding | 5–15 years

Make It Move

Kids build their own interactive games, stories, and animations — independently, from an empty canvas.

  • Design and code an original Scratch project
  • Apply logic to make characters and scenes actually work
  • Solve bugs on their own — no hand-holding
  • Present and explain every choice they made
TinkerCAD · 3D Design | 5–15 years

Make It Real

Kids design 3D objects from scratch — the same tool NASA engineers start with. Spatial thinking made tangible.

  • Build original 3D shapes and structures
  • Think spatially — width, depth, balance
  • Create something that could be 3D printed
  • 🚀 NASA starts here. So will your child.
Creative Writing · Storytelling | 5–15 years

Write Your Book

Every child has a story inside them. We give them the structure, the voice, and the courage to put it on a page.

  • Develop characters, plot, and a real story arc
  • Write a short illustrated book — cover to last page
  • Edit, refine, and own their words
  • Build a writing habit that lasts a lifetime
Art · Observation · Sketching | 5–15 years

Sketch Anyone

Learn to see before you draw. Kids develop the art of observation and translate it into confident, expressive portraits.

  • Understand proportions, light, and shadow
  • Sketch faces, expressions, and characters
  • Develop a personal drawing style
  • Turn any inspiration into a handmade portrait
Every Course · Every Day

The Creator Mindset

Woven into everything we do. The habits that separate managers from makers — taught from day one.

  • Work independently without being prompted
  • Embrace mistakes as a core part of building
  • Ask better questions instead of waiting for answers
  • Feel genuine pride and ownership in their work
